# Day 54 — Business Visualization Summary

## Objective
Translate customer analytics into visual insights that support executive decision-making.

## 1. Customer Risk Distribution
**Chart:** `day54_risk_distribution.png`

- Medium Risk: 264 customers (33.29%).

**Business insight:** Approximately one-third of customers belong to the high-risk segment, making it an important group for further review.

## 2. Sales by Risk Level
**Chart:** `day54_sales_by_risk.png`

- High Risk: ₹598,837.94 (26.48% of sales).
- Medium Risk: ₹766,988.50 (33.91% of sales).
- Low Risk: ₹895,710.34 (39.61% of sales).

**Business insight:** Low-risk customers contribute the largest share of recorded sales, while high-risk customers contribute 26.48%.

## 3. Purchase Recency by Risk Level
**Chart:** `day54_recency_by_risk.png`

- High Risk: 345.04 average days since last order.
- Medium Risk: 77.56 average days.
- Low Risk: 22.74 average days.

**Business insight:** The high-risk group has substantially longer purchase gaps. Further analysis can help identify suitable re-engagement opportunities.

## 4. Retention Strategy Distribution
**Chart:** `day54_retention_strategies.png`

- Maintain Relationship: 265 customers.
- Engagement Campaign: 264 customers.
- Targeted Re-engagement: 254 customers.
- Immediate Retention Campaign: 10 customers.

**Business insight:** The strategy distribution is associated with the existing risk categories. Management can review whether the assigned strategies suit each customer segment.

## Executive Takeaway
The visualizations indicate differences in customer sales and purchase recency across risk categories. These patterns can inform retention experiments and prioritisation, but they do not prove that a particular intervention will improve revenue.
