# Day 31 – Advanced Customer Insights

## Objective
Transform customer clustering results into meaningful customer
personas and business strategy recommendations.

## Dataset
- 100 synthetic customers
- Features: Age, Income, Purchase Amount, Savings, Credit Score
- KMeans clustering with 6 clusters

## Tasks Completed
1. Analyzed customer clusters.
2. Created six business-friendly customer personas.
3. Calculated customer percentages and simulated purchase rates.
4. Visualized spending and purchase patterns.
5. Developed business strategy recommendations.

## Key Findings
- High-Savings Active Buyers had the highest average purchase amount.
- Mature Value-Conscious Customers had the lowest average purchase amount.
- Affluent Active Spenders had a simulated purchase rate of 100%.

## Business Recommendations
Personalize promotions, loyalty programs, premium offers,
and product recommendations according to customer personas.

## Files
- day31_customer_personas.ipynb
- day31_customer_persona_report.csv
- day31_business_strategy_recommendations.csv

## Limitations
The dataset is synthetic and contains only 100 customers.
Purchase behavior is simulated, not based on real transactions.
Business recommendations are hypotheses that require real-world testing.

## Tools
Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, Scikit-learn, KMeans
