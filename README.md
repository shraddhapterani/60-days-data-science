# Superstore Sales Analysis — 60 Days of Data Science

## Problem Statement
This project analyzes sales data from a retail superstore to uncover which products, 
regions, and customer segments drive the most revenue and profit. The goal is to 
identify patterns in sales performance, understand the impact of discounts on 
profitability, and generate insights that could inform better business decisions.

## Dataset
- Source: Kaggle — Superstore Sales Dataset
- Features: Order details, product categories, sales, profit, quantity, customer 
  segments, shipping info, regional data

## Tools
Python, Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn

## Goals
- Identify top-performing products, categories, and regions
- Understand the relationship between discounts and profit
- Explore monthly/seasonal sales trends
- Build visualizations to communicate findings clearly
