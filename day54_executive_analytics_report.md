# Day 54 — Executive Analytics Report
## Turning Analytics into Business Insights

### 1. Executive Summary

The customer analytics dataset contains 793 customers with total recorded sales of ₹2,261,536.78 and total estimated Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) of ₹2,556,306.97.

The analysis identifies 264 high-risk customers (33.29%). These customers have the longest average purchase gap, at 345.04 days, making them a priority group for further investigation and potential re-engagement.

### 2. Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total customers | 793 |
| Total recorded sales | ₹2,261,536.78 |
| Total estimated CLV | ₹2,556,306.97 |
| High-risk customers | 264 |
| High-risk customer percentage | 33.29% |

### 3. Customer Risk Findings

- Low Risk: 265 customers (33.42%).
- Medium Risk: 264 customers (33.29%).
- High Risk: 264 customers (33.29%).

The near-equal distribution across risk groups means the business should review each segment's behaviour instead of relying on overall customer counts alone.

### 4. Sales and Customer Value

| Risk Group | Total Sales | Sales Share | Estimated CLV Share |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| High Risk | ₹598,837.94 | 26.48% | 31.33% |
| Medium Risk | ₹766,988.50 | 33.91% | 32.24% |
| Low Risk | ₹895,710.34 | 39.61% | 36.43% |

High-risk customers account for 31.33% of estimated CLV. Their value should be considered when planning retention activities, while recognising that estimated CLV is not guaranteed future revenue.

### 5. Purchase Recency Findings

| Risk Group | Average Days Since Last Order |
|---|---:|
| High Risk | 345.04 |
| Medium Risk | 77.56 |
| Low Risk | 22.74 |

High-risk customers have substantially longer purchase gaps than the other groups. This pattern supports investigating targeted re-engagement opportunities.

### 6. Retention Strategy Findings

| Retention Strategy | Customers |
|---|---:|
| Maintain Relationship | 265 |
| Engagement Campaign | 264 |
| Targeted Re-engagement | 254 |
| Immediate Retention Campaign | 10 |

All 264 high-risk customers are assigned to either Targeted Re-engagement (254) or Immediate Retention Campaign (10). Medium-risk customers are assigned to Engagement Campaign, while low-risk customers are assigned to Maintain Relationship.

### 7. Recommended Business Actions

1. Review high-risk customers with long purchase gaps.
2. Test targeted re-engagement campaigns with measurable success criteria.
3. Prioritise outreach using risk, estimated CLV, and relevant customer context.
4. Track repeat purchases, recovered sales, and campaign response.
5. Compare campaign outcomes against a baseline before scaling interventions.

### 8. Limitations

The analysis describes patterns in the available dataset. It does not prove that customers will churn or that a particular campaign will improve revenue. Campaign effectiveness must be assessed using subsequent customer data.

### 9. Conclusion

Customer analytics becomes more useful when technical outputs are translated into business priorities. This analysis highlights the high-risk segment, its purchase inactivity, and its share of estimated customer value to guide further investigation and retention experiments.
