# Day 42 - Unified Business Intelligence System

## Objective

Build a unified Business Intelligence workflow by integrating customer analytics, KPI tracking, predictive risk scoring, CLV analysis, and retention strategies.

## What I Built

- Customer-level business intelligence analysis
- Executive KPI summary
- Customer risk scoring
- Customer risk classification
- Customer Lifetime Value (CLV) analysis
- Automated retention strategies
- High-risk customer identification
- Business decision insights
- Unified BI architecture workflow
